This policy explains what Mechi collects, why, who it is shared with, and the rights you have under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. We do not sell your personal data or use advertising trackers.

Who we are

Mechi is a dating app for adults in Kenya, run by Muskari IT Solutions ("Mechi", "we", "us"). We decide how your personal data is used, which makes us the data controller under the Data Protection Act, 2019.

What other users can see

  • People nearby see your first name, age, gender, bio, photos, and your distance from them rounded to the nearest kilometre.
  • Only your matches see the messages you exchange with each of them.
  • Nobody else ever sees your surname, email address, exact date of birth, exact location, who you liked or passed on, who you blocked, reports you made, or your payment details.

People you block, and people who block you, stop seeing each other anywhere in the app. Nobody is told when you block, report or pass on them. Anything you send in a chat can be seen, copied or shared by the person you send it to, so share personal details with care.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored outside Kenya, in the European Union: our database and photos in Ireland, and our server in Germany. Both are covered by the EU's General Data Protection Regulation. We transfer data there under the safeguards the Data Protection Act requires, relying on our providers' data processing agreements and security measures.

Notifications are passed on by Expo and Google, and the app download is hosted by GitHub. These providers may process limited technical information, such as your phone's notification address and the notification text, in the United States and other countries where they operate, under their own data protection safeguards. No profile details, photos or chat messages are sent to them.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile, Settings, Delete my account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page.

When you delete your account, we immediately and permanently delete your profile, photos, location, likes and passes, matches, the messages in your chats (for both people), blocks, reports linked to you, and your Premium payment records. Copies in our providers' backups may remain for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.

  • Unmatching deletes that conversation for both people.
  • Blocking or reporting hides the conversation from both people but keeps it, so a report can be reviewed. It is deleted when either account is deleted.
  • Removing a photo deletes the photo file.
  • Your phone's notification address is deleted when you log out, delete your account, or uninstall Mechi.
  • Unverified sign-ups, where the email was never confirmed, may be deleted after one day if someone else signs up with that email address.

How we protect it

  • All data travels between the app and our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Passwords and email codes are stored only in scrambled form, so we cannot read them.
  • Your login is kept in your phone's encrypted storage.
  • Only you can change your own profile, photos and chats, and only matched people can message each other.
  • Our database is closed to direct public access; only the Mechi server can read it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at real risk, we will notify you and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as the law requires.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used;
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is wrong or misleading (you can edit your bio, photos and preferences in the app);
  • delete your data (you can delete your whole account in the app);
  • object to how we use your data, and withdraw consent, for example for location;
  • move your data, by asking for a copy in a common format.

To use any of these rights, email privacy@mechyapp.com from the address on your account. We will reply within the time the law requires and may ask you to confirm your identity first. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Adults only

Mechi is only for people aged 18 and over. We check your date of birth at sign-up and do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we learn that a user is under 18, we delete the account. Read our child safety standards.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will ask you to read and agree to the new version in the app before you can keep using it. The effective date at the top of this page always shows the current version.
